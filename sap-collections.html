SAP Collections: An AI Agent That Calls Your Overdue Customers

Finance teams on SAP get Jess without a long implementation project. She works from the same receivables your team sees, calls the customers who owe you, follows up on WhatsApp and email, and sends the documents that hold up payment.

How do you automate collections in SAP?

Seenn connects to SAP through a ready-made integration, in days rather than months. Its AI agent, Jess, works your open receivables, calls each overdue customer in a natural voice, follows up on WhatsApp and email by your collection policy, and sends invoices, statements and receipts from SAP herself. Your team keeps working in SAP as usual.

What does SAP do for collections on its own?

SAP organizes collections; it does not contact the customer. SAP Business One has the Dunning Wizard, which issues dunning letters by level, with fees and interest. SAP S/4HANA runs dunning (F150, or the Create Dunning Notices app), and its Collections Management adds the Process Collections Worklist app, a prioritized list of customers for your collection specialists to contact.

The contact itself is still made by people: someone dials, explains, resends the invoice and writes down what the customer promised. That is the work Jess takes on.

What does Jess do with your SAP receivables?

SAP knows exactly who owes what and since when. What it does not do is pick up the phone. Jess does the outreach that otherwise falls to your collectors:

  • Calls first. Jess phones overdue customers in a natural voice, under your company name, handles their answers and ends each call with a next step.
  • Follows up in writing. On official WhatsApp Business from your verified number (Seenn is an approved Meta Tech Provider) and by email under your brand, with a payment link for the exact balance.
  • Sends the paperwork. When a customer asks for an invoice, a statement or a receipt, Jess pulls it from SAP and sends it on the spot.
  • Works by your rules. You set payment terms, grace periods, tone, channel order and when to escalate; she applies them to every account and hands you only what needs a decision.
  • Keeps the record. Every call, message, promise to pay and dispute is logged. The dashboard shows total and overdue receivables, aging that matches your data, what falls due by month and what was collected each month.

How do you connect SAP to Seenn?

Three ways, depending on how fast you want to start and how much you want connected:

  1. Ready-made integration. Seenn connects to SAP directly, in days rather than months. Jess works from your open receivables and pulls invoices, statements and receipts herself.
  2. Excel upload. Export your open invoices from SAP (steps below), upload the file, and Jess starts within minutes. You can switch to the direct connection later.
  3. Custom integration or API. For enterprise teams with several entities, custom fields or their own workflows.

How do you export open invoices from SAP?

With the ready-made integration you don't need to export anything. To start the same day, or to try Jess before connecting, export your open receivables:

SAP Business One

  1. Go to Financials > Financial Reports > Accounting > Aging > Customer Receivables Aging.
  2. Select all customers, set the Aging Date to today and age by Due Date.
  3. Leave Ignore Future Remit unchecked, so invoices that are not yet due stay in the list.
  4. Run the report, click the Microsoft Excel icon on the toolbar, and upload the file to Seenn.

SAP S/4HANA

Open the Manage Customer Line Items app (or transaction FBL5N in SAP GUI), show open items at today's key date, and export the list to a spreadsheet. Note that the export leaves out subtotals and totals.

Jess needs a phone number and an email for each customer. If the report you export has no contact columns, add them from your customer list before you upload.

SAP collections FAQ

Does Seenn integrate with SAP?

Yes. SAP is one of Seenn's ready-made integrations, alongside Priority, Hashavshevet and iCount/Rivhit, and the connection takes days, not months. You can also start from an Excel export and connect later.

Do I need SAP's Collections Management module?

No. If you already use it, Jess complements it: SAP builds the worklist, and Jess does the calling and the follow-ups. If you don't, Jess works straight from your open receivables, prioritizing accounts by your rules.

Which documents can Jess send from SAP?

Invoices, account statements and receipts, the moment a customer asks for them on a call, on WhatsApp or by email. Paperwork disputes close without involving your team.

Does my team need to change how it works in SAP?

No. Your team keeps invoicing and posting payments in SAP as usual. Jess works alongside it from the same receivables data and hands your team only the accounts that need a decision.

Which channels does Jess use?

Phone first, in a natural voice. She follows up on official WhatsApp Business from your verified number and by email under your brand, with a payment link for the exact balance. Seenn does not use SMS.

Is our SAP data shared with anyone?

No. Jess learns how your own customers pay so she can collect better for you. Your data stays yours and is never shared with or used for any other business.
