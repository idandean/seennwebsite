What does Shira do with your accounting system?

Most overdue invoices aren't stuck because the client refuses to pay. They're stuck on paperwork. With a direct integration, Shira pulls the right document from your system and sends it inside the conversation, so the excuse gets answered in seconds instead of landing back on your desk. That's how Seenn's collections software collects instead of just reminding.

  • "Resend the invoice" → Shira pulls it from your system and sends it on the spot.
  • "I already paid" → she checks and sends back the receipt or account statement.
  • "What do I owe?" → she sends an up-to-date ledger card with every open item.
  • Every call, message and outcome is logged to your Seenn dashboard.

Larger finance teams rarely run a single off-the-shelf system. If you have an in-house ERP, several entities on different systems, or specific data requirements, we build a custom integration or API connection around your setup, so Shira works from the same numbers your team does.
