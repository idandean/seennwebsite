What is a WhatsApp payment reminder template?

A WhatsApp payment reminder template is a prepared message that tells a customer an invoice is coming due or overdue. On the WhatsApp Business Platform, a reminder you start yourself must use a Meta-approved template in the utility category, with variables such as {{1}} for the customer name, invoice number, amount, due date and payment link.

When should you send each WhatsApp payment reminder?

A reminder ladder sends a short, specific message at each stage of an invoice and gets firmer as the invoice ages. The early messages are friendly and assume the customer means to pay. The later ones ask for a date and name the next step. Stop the ladder as soon as the invoice is paid or the customer commits to a date.

How to use these templates

  • Sending through the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API): submit the Meta format versions as utility templates. Meta uses numbered variables, {{1}}, {{2}} and so on, filled in for each customer when the message goes out.
  • Sending by hand from the WhatsApp Business app: use the plain versions and replace each {placeholder} before you send.
  • One invoice per message. If a customer has several open invoices, send the account statement instead and give the total.

What are the WhatsApp Business rules for payment reminders?

  • Outside the 24-hour window, use an approved template. You can send free-form messages only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Any reminder you start yourself has to use a template Meta has approved.
  • Submit payment reminders as utility templates. Utility covers messages about an existing transaction, such as an invoice coming due. Keep them transactional: invoice number, amount, due date and payment link.
  • Leave out anything promotional. A discount, a new product or a newsletter link can get the template categorized as marketing, which changes how it is priced and delivered.
  • Message only customers who opted in. Meta requires opt-in before a business starts a WhatsApp conversation. For business customers, collect it on the credit application, order form or onboarding paperwork, and keep a record.
  • Keep variables inside sentences. Avoid starting or ending the body with a variable, since Meta can reject templates that do. Add a sample value for each variable when you submit the template.
  • Consider a URL button for the link. If your payment links share a base address, a button with a dynamic suffix can replace the link in the body.

Meta updates its WhatsApp Business policies and pricing from time to time. Check the current rules in Meta's WhatsApp Business documentation before you submit templates.

What do you do when the customer replies?

A reply opens a 24-hour window in which you can answer freely, without a template. Most replies fall into a few types:

  • “We already paid.” Ask for the payment date or reference and send the account statement so they can compare.
  • “Can you send the invoice?” Send the PDF in the same thread, with the payment link.
  • “We will pay Friday.” Confirm the date in writing and follow up on Friday if the payment has not arrived.
  • “There is a problem with the invoice.” Ask what is wrong, send the supporting documents and pass it to whoever can fix it.

WhatsApp payment reminder templates FAQ

Can I send payment reminders on WhatsApp Business?

Yes. From the WhatsApp Business app you can message customers by hand. Through the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), any reminder you start outside a 24-hour reply window must use a Meta-approved template, usually in the utility category. Once the customer replies, you can answer freely for 24 hours.

Which template category should a WhatsApp payment reminder use?

Utility. Payment reminders are transactional messages about an existing invoice. Include the invoice number, amount, due date and payment link, and keep promotional content out, since it can get the template categorized as marketing.

Do I need the customer's opt-in to send WhatsApp reminders?

Yes. Meta requires opt-in before a business starts a WhatsApp conversation. For business customers, the simplest place to collect it is the credit application, order form or onboarding paperwork. Keep a record of it.

What variables should a WhatsApp invoice reminder include?

The customer's name, your company name, the invoice number, the amount, the due date and a payment link. A final notice also needs the deadline date. Number them {{1}}, {{2}} and so on in the order they appear, keep them inside sentences, and give Meta a sample value for each.

How many WhatsApp reminders should I send for one invoice?

A six-step ladder covers the full life of an invoice: 3 days before the due date, the due date, then 7, 14 and 30 days late, and a final notice. Most invoices will not need every step. If a customer stops answering messages, switch to a phone call.
