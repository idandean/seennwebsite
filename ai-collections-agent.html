What is an AI collections agent?

An AI collections agent is software that works your overdue invoices the way a collector would. It contacts each customer who owes you, holds a two-way conversation, answers objections, sends the documents they need and follows up until the invoice is paid. Seenn's agent, Jess, phones first, then follows up on WhatsApp and email.

How is an AI collections agent different from reminder software?

Most tools sold as collections automation are schedulers. They send an email on the due date, another a week later, and stop the moment the customer writes back. From there the reply is yours: find the invoice, check whether the payment came in, answer the question and decide what happens next.

An AI collections agent, sometimes called an AI accounts receivable agent, handles the reply itself. When a customer says they never got the invoice, it sends the invoice. When they say they already paid, it checks and sends the receipt or the account statement. When they promise a date, it records the promise and follows up on that date.

  • It understands replies. “We already paid”, “send it again” and “Friday works” each lead to a different action.
  • It sends the paperwork. Invoices, account statements and receipts come straight from your accounting system.
  • It decides the next step. When to follow up, on which channel, and when to hand the account to a person, based on your rules.
  • It works the whole ledger. Jess reaches the right accounts at the right time, sometimes before the due date, instead of waiting for someone to notice.
  • It learns your customers. Jess learns how your own customers pay so she can collect better for you. Your data stays yours and is never shared with or used for anyone else.

Which channels does an AI collections agent use?

Jess reaches each customer on the channel they actually answer, and the phone call carries the most weight. Almost no collections software places real calls, yet the conversations that move a stuck invoice usually happen by voice.

  • Phone: AI collection calls in a natural voice under your company name. Jess handles objections, sends a payment link for the exact balance and ends each call with a next step.
  • WhatsApp: messages through the official WhatsApp Business API from a verified business number (Seenn is an approved Meta Tech Provider), with the invoice number, the exact amount and a payment link. When the customer replies, Jess answers.
  • Email: follow-ups under your brand with the invoice attached and a payment link. Jess reads the replies and acts on them.

You choose the channel order, timing and tone. Jess runs the sequence and switches channel when a customer stays quiet.

How does an AI collections agent work?

  1. Connect your receivables. Use a ready-made integration with Priority, SAP, Hashavshevet or iCount/Rivhit, upload an Excel file from any system, or have us connect QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or another system on request.
  2. Set your rules. Payment terms, grace periods, contact schedule, channel order, tone and escalation rules, including when a person should take over.
  3. Jess starts collecting. She contacts each open account at the right time, holds the conversations, sends documents and payment links, and records every promise and dispute.
  4. You see everything. The dashboard shows total and overdue receivables, aging by day ranges or by month depending on your data, what falls due each month and what was collected each month. Every call, message, promise and dispute is logged.

AI collections agent FAQ

What is an AI collections agent?

An AI collections agent is software that works your overdue invoices the way a collector would. It contacts each customer who owes you, holds a two-way conversation, answers objections, sends the documents they need and follows up until the invoice is paid. Seenn's agent, Jess, phones first, then follows up on WhatsApp and email.

How is an AI collections agent different from dunning or reminder software?

Reminder and dunning software sends scheduled messages and stops when the customer replies, so the reply lands back on your desk. An AI collections agent reads the reply and acts on it: it sends the invoice when the customer never got it, checks and sends a receipt or statement when they say they paid, records a promise to pay, and hands the account to a person only when it needs one.

Does Seenn's AI collections agent make phone calls?

Yes, and it is the channel Jess leads with. She calls overdue customers in a natural voice under your company name, from a dedicated number or your own, and holds a real conversation. During the call she can send a payment link for the exact balance and the documents the customer asks for, then follow up on WhatsApp or by email.

Will an AI collections agent hurt my customer relationships?

Jess contacts customers politely and professionally, under your brand, in the tone and on the schedule you set. When a conversation needs a person, she hands it to your team with the history attached. Customers get a timely, specific follow-up instead of a tense call after two months of silence.

Does it work with QuickBooks, Xero or NetSuite?

Yes, on request. Seenn connects QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Sage, Odoo and other systems on request. The ready-made integrations today are Priority, SAP, Hashavshevet and iCount/Rivhit. You can start the same day by exporting open invoices from any system to Excel, which takes minutes to set up.

Does an AI collections agent replace my AR team?

No. It takes over the chasing: the calls, reminders, document requests and follow-ups on promises. Your team sets the rules, sees everything in the dashboard and handles the accounts Jess hands over. That is how businesses using Seenn save 10+ hours a week without hiring another collector.
