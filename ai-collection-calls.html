AI Collection Calls: A Voice Agent That Calls About Unpaid Invoices

Jess, Seenn's AI voice agent, phones customers about unpaid invoices in a natural voice under your company name. She answers questions and objections, sends a payment link or the missing document, and ends every call with a payment date or a scheduled follow-up.

What are AI collection calls?

AI collection calls are phone calls about unpaid invoices placed by an AI voice agent that holds a two-way conversation. The agent states the invoice and balance, listens, answers objections, sends a payment link or document during the call and records the outcome. At Seenn the agent is Jess, and the phone is her first channel.

Why does a phone call move overdue invoices?

An email is easy to leave unread. A call gets an answer, and the answer is usually the reason the invoice is stuck: it never reached accounts payable, it's waiting on an approval, or someone believes it was paid. Most of those can be cleared in the same conversation.

The problem is time. Calling every overdue account by hand takes hours, so most teams call only the largest balances, or wait until an invoice is badly overdue. Jess calls every account on your list when your rules say to, and almost no other collections software places real calls.

How are AI collection calls different from automated reminder calls?

Automated payment reminder calls have been around for years. A robodialer plays a recorded message, or a text-to-speech system reads out the amount and due date, and the call ends. Some offer a keypad menu to pay. If the customer has a question, nobody's there to answer it, so the question turns into an email to your team or into silence.

An AI voice agent listens and responds. When the customer says “we never got that invoice”, Jess sends it. When they say “we paid last week”, she sends the account statement so they can match it. When they say “call me Thursday”, she calls Thursday. The call ends with a result you can act on.

What does Jess do on a call?

  • Calls in a natural voice under your company name, from a dedicated number or your own.
  • States the invoice number, amount and due date, and asks for payment.
  • Handles the common objections, “we never got it”, “we already paid”, “what do we owe?”, and sends the invoice, account statement or receipt on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Sends a payment link for the exact balance while the customer is still on the line.
  • Gets a commitment to a payment date and sets the follow-up herself.
  • Hands the call to a person when it needs one, following your rules.
  • Logs the call, the promise or the dispute in the Seenn dashboard.

What happens after the call?

Most invoices take more than one touch. If the customer asked for a document, Jess sends it on WhatsApp or by email. If nobody answered, she tries again later or switches channel, according to your contact schedule. If the customer promised a date, she checks whether the payment came in and follows up if it did not. Escalation follows the rules you set, and everything goes out under your company name.

That makes the call one step in the work of an AI collections agent that carries each invoice from overdue to paid. If your team makes some calls itself, our collection call scripts cover the objections Jess hears most.

AI collection calls FAQ

Does Jess sound natural on the phone?

Yes. Jess speaks in a natural, human-sounding voice and holds a real conversation: she listens, understands and responds to what the customer says. You can talk to her yourself on the Seenn homepage, right in your browser.

Which number does Jess call from?

You can get a dedicated phone number or use your own. Either way, Jess calls under your company name.

What happens if the customer disputes the invoice?

Jess listens, records the dispute and sends the supporting documents, such as the invoice or the account statement, on WhatsApp or by email. If the customer says they already paid, she checks and sends the receipt or the statement. A dispute that needs a decision goes to your team with the full history attached.

When does a person take over?

When the call needs one. You set the rules, for example a disputed charge, a request for a payment plan or a customer who asks to speak with someone, and Jess hands off with the conversation logged, so your team picks up where she left off.

Does Jess call outside business hours?

Jess works around the clock across your ledger, but she calls only within the calling hours and contact schedule you set. You decide how often she tries an account and when she switches to WhatsApp or email. Check your own obligations for business calls with counsel.
