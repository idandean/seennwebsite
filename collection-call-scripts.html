What is a collection call script?

A collection call script is a short, prepared outline for phoning a customer about a past due invoice. It covers how to open the call, the exact invoice and amount to state, answers to the common objections, and how to close with a specific next step: a payment date, a document to send or a follow-up call.

How should a B2B collection call be structured?

Every call in this guide follows the same six steps. The scripts below fill in the words for the moments that usually derail a call.

  1. Identify yourself and confirm you are speaking with the right person.
  2. State the facts: invoice number, amount and due date.
  3. Ask a direct question about where the payment stands.
  4. Listen and handle the objection you hear.
  5. Agree on a specific next step: a payment date, a document to send or a time to call back.
  6. Confirm it in writing the same day.

These scripts are written for business-to-business invoices on net terms. They are not legal advice; check your obligations with counsel.

Opening script for a past due invoice call

I wanted to check where it stands. Is it scheduled for payment?

[If no] Is something holding it up that I can help with?

How do you make collection calls more effective?

  • Keep the tone calm and specific. Assume a process problem first. Late B2B invoices are often stuck on paperwork or an approval step, and a polite question gets you to the cause faster.
  • Have the facts in front of you. Invoice number, amount, due date, PO number and the notes from your last contact.
  • Document every call. Date, who you spoke with, what they said and what was agreed. The next call starts from those notes.
  • End with a next step. A call that ends with “I'll look into it” and no date is a call you'll have to make again.
  • Confirm in writing the same day. A short email with the amount, the date and a payment link turns a verbal promise into a record.
  • Call at sensible times. Stay within business hours in the customer's time zone and follow a consistent contact schedule.

Collection call scripts FAQ

What should you say on a collection call?

Identify yourself and your company, state the invoice number, amount and due date, and ask where the payment stands. Then listen, handle the objection, agree on a specific next step such as a payment date, and confirm it in writing the same day.

How do you start a call about a past due invoice?

Confirm you have the right person, then say who you are, which company you are calling from, and which invoice you are calling about, with the amount and due date. Follow with a direct question: is it scheduled for payment?

What do you say in a voicemail about an overdue invoice?

Keep it short: your name, your company, the invoice number, the amount and the due date, a callback number said twice, and a note that you are also sending the invoice and a payment link by email.

How do you respond when a customer says they already paid?

Thank them, say it still shows as open on your side, and ask for the payment date, amount or reference. If they do not have it, send an account statement listing every open invoice and payment so both sides can compare, and agree on when you will check back.

What is a promise to pay, and how do you confirm it?

A promise to pay is the customer's commitment to pay a specific amount by a specific date. Confirm the amount, date and payment method on the call, send a short written confirmation with a payment link the same day, and follow up if the payment has not arrived by that date.
