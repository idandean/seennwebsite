A Chaser Alternative for WhatsApp-First and Hebrew-Speaking Businesses

Chaser is a capable, established AR tool with a wide channel mix. Where Seenn differs is the channel your clients actually reply on — official WhatsApp Business — plus a Hebrew agent and the Israeli accounting systems Chaser does not name.

Seenn is an AI accounts-receivable agent that collects on the channels clients answer. Both tools automate email reminders and voice calls. Seenn adds official WhatsApp Business messaging as an approved Meta Tech Provider, a Hebrew-speaking agent, and integrations with Priority and Hashavshevet — none of which Chaser lists on its site.

When Chaser is the better choice

Chaser has been at this longer and it shows. There are real cases where it is the better fit.

  • ✓You run Xero, QuickBooks Online or Sage and want an integration that has been in production for years.
  • ✓You need physical letters as an escalation step — Seenn does not send post.
  • ✓You want SMS as a distinct channel rather than WhatsApp.
  • ✓You are buying on an established track record and a large public user base.

When Seenn is the better choice

Seenn is the better fit when the reply comes back on WhatsApp, or when the ledger is in Hebrew.

  • ✓Your clients answer WhatsApp and ignore email — and you need the official Business API from a verified number, not a personal phone.
  • ✓You invoice in Israel and run Priority or Hashavshevet.
  • ✓You need the agent to speak Hebrew to your clients, not just to you.
  • ✓You want the loop closed: the reply handled, the payment link sent, the outcome recorded — not just a reminder delivered.

Seenn vs Chaser FAQ

Does Chaser send WhatsApp messages?

Not according to its own site, which lists email, SMS, auto-call, in-app phone calls and physical letters as its channels. Seenn sends on the official WhatsApp Business API as an approved Meta Tech Provider. Checked on chaserhq.com, 5 August 2026.

Does Chaser make automated phone calls?

Yes. Its site lists an “Auto-call” feature for automated voice calls, alongside in-app calling for your own team. This is a place where the two products genuinely overlap, so compare them on the quality of the conversation rather than on whether a call happens at all.

Is Seenn cheaper than Chaser?

Chaser publishes pricing on its pricing page; Seenn prices around your receivables volume and quotes on a demo. Get both numbers for your own volume before deciding — a per-seat price and a volume price are not comparable in the abstract.

Which one integrates with my accounting system?

Chaser names a broad list including Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage, NetSuite, SAP, Dynamics 365 Business Central and Odoo. Seenn integrates with Priority, SAP and Hashavshevet, and offers custom integrations and an API. On Xero or QuickBooks, Chaser has the longer-standing integration; on Priority or Hashavshevet, Chaser does not name them.

Can either tool handle a reply or a dispute?

Seenn’s agent answers replies in the thread — questions about which months a balance covers, “I already paid”, requests to re-send the invoice — and transfers the call to you when it needs a person. Compare this directly in a demo of both; it is where automated chasing and actual collecting diverge.

Does Seenn send physical letters?

No. Seenn works phone, WhatsApp and email. If letters are a required escalation step in your process, Chaser offers them and Seenn does not.
