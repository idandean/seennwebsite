When is Paidnice the better choice?

Paidnice is a strong, low-cost tool for small businesses whose books live in Xero or QuickBooks Online. It is the better fit when you:

  • want a published price you can sign up for today, starting at US$69 a month,
  • need late fees, statutory interest or payment plans applied automatically,
  • want letters by post as part of the escalation,
  • are happy for your own team to make the phone calls.

When is Seenn the better choice?

Seenn is the better fit when the calls are the part nobody has time for. Pick Seenn if you:

  • want the AI to make the calls: Jess phones your customers in a natural voice and ends each call with a next step,
  • want replies handled on the spot, such as sending the invoice the moment a customer says they never got it,
  • want follow-ups on official WhatsApp Business from your verified number,
  • run an accounting system other than Xero or QuickBooks, or want to start from an Excel export today,
  • collect from customers in Israel as well as the US.

Does Paidnice call customers for you?

No AI calling is described on its site. Paidnice lets your team place calls from inside the app, or rings your phone and connects you to the customer, and on Pro it records, transcribes and summarizes those calls. Seenn works the other way round: Jess makes the call, and you step in only when she hands the call over.

Seenn vs Paidnice FAQ

What is the difference between Seenn and Paidnice?

Paidnice enforces payment terms for small businesses on Xero and QuickBooks Online, with reminders, late fees, payment plans and calls placed by your own team. Seenn is an AI collections agent: Jess phones overdue customers herself, follows up on official WhatsApp and email, sends the documents customers ask for and keeps going until the invoice is paid.

Does Seenn work with Xero and QuickBooks?

Yes, on request. Tell us you run Xero or QuickBooks and we connect it for you. You can also start the same day by exporting your open invoices to Excel and uploading them to Seenn.

Does Seenn charge late fees like Paidnice?

No. Seenn does not add late fees or interest to invoices. It focuses on getting the customer to pay: the calls, the WhatsApp and email follow-ups, the documents they ask for, and a record of every promise to pay and dispute.

Is Seenn more expensive than Paidnice?

Paidnice publishes plans starting at US$69 a month. Seenn builds a custom plan around your receivables volume, because it includes an agent that makes the calls. Request a demo to get a quote for your ledger.

Can Seenn send SMS reminders?

No. Seenn uses three channels: phone calls, official WhatsApp Business and email. The phone call comes first, because it is the one customers answer.

Does Seenn work in Hebrew?

Yes. Jess speaks English and Hebrew, and the Seenn site and product ship in both languages, so one team can collect from customers in the US and Israel.
