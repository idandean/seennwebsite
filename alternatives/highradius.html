When HighRadius is the better choice

Be honest with yourself about size. HighRadius is a serious enterprise platform and there are cases where it is simply the right answer.

  • ✓You are an enterprise or upper mid-market finance organisation with a dedicated collections team.
  • ✓You need cash application, deductions, credit management, treasury and financial close in one platform — not just collections.
  • ✓You run a large enterprise ERP and need deep integration across the whole order-to-cash cycle.
  • ✓You have the implementation capacity a suite of that size expects.

When Seenn is the better choice

Seenn is the answer when collections is the problem and you do not have a collections department to solve it with.

  • ✓You want the calls actually made, not a dialer for a person you have not hired.
  • ✓Your clients answer WhatsApp faster than email — and you need the official Business API, not someone’s personal number.
  • ✓You run Priority, Hashavshevet or SAP in Israel and need Hebrew end to end.
  • ✓You want to be collecting in days, without an enterprise implementation project.

Seenn vs HighRadius FAQ

Is Seenn a real HighRadius alternative?

For collections, yes. For the rest of the office of the CFO, no — and that is deliberate. HighRadius sells cash application, deductions, credit, treasury and financial close alongside collections. Seenn does collections only. If you need the suite, buy the suite; if you need overdue invoices chased and closed, Seenn is built for exactly that.

Does HighRadius make automated AI phone calls?

Its site describes an “In App Dialer” for collections calls, which is a tool that helps a human agent make the call. It does not describe automated AI voice calling. Seenn’s agent places the call herself in a natural voice and handles what comes back. Checked on highradius.com, 5 August 2026.

How much does HighRadius cost compared with Seenn?

HighRadius publishes an outcome-based model — no implementation fee, nothing until go-live, then a share of the gain — but no rates. Seenn prices around your receivables volume and quotes on a demo. Neither is a public list price, so compare them on a call with both.

Is Seenn big enough to trust with our receivables?

Seenn is a young company and does not pretend otherwise. What it can show you is your own ledger, worked by the agent, in a demo — real overdue invoices, the actual calls and messages, and the outcomes written back. That is a better basis for a decision than a logo wall.

Do you integrate with the same ERPs as HighRadius?

Not the same list. Seenn integrates with Priority, SAP and Hashavshevet, and offers custom integrations and an API. HighRadius covers a broader set of large-enterprise ERPs. If your system is not on our list, ask — the API exists for that reason.

Can Seenn work in Hebrew?

Yes. The interface, the agent and the site all ship in Hebrew as well as English, and Seenn integrates with Israeli accounting systems. HighRadius does not offer Hebrew as a product language on its site.
