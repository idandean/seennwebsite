What is Seenn collections software?

Why do reminder tools stop working the moment a client replies?

Picture the last overdue invoice you chased. You sent the polite email. Silence. You sent the firmer one. Then the reply landed: "I already paid that" — or "can you resend it?" — or "we'll sort it next week." And just like that, it was back on your desk. The reminder tool fired the nudge, but the second a human replied, it went quiet and handed you the awkward part.

That's the gap every accounts receivable automation tool leaves open. Sending reminders is easy. The conversation that actually unlocks payment — answering the objection, pulling the invoice, holding the client to the promise — is the hard part, and it's exactly the part most collections software dumps right back on you.

Seenn was built to close that gap. It's AR automation that keeps going when the reply comes in. That one difference is the whole point: reminder tools remind. Seenn collects.

  • Reminder tools: send a nudge, then stop and wait for you to take over.
  • Seenn: sends the nudge, handles the reply, sends the documents, resolves the dispute, takes the payment, records the outcome — on its own.

She sends the invoices, ledger cards and receipts herself — so disputes resolve without you

This is where Seenn really earns its keep. Most of the time an invoice isn't unpaid because the client won't pay — it's stalled on a piece of missing paperwork. "Resend the invoice." "I already paid that." "What do I even owe you?" With a reminder tool, every one of those replies bounces straight back to your desk. With Jess, none of them do.

Jess connects to your accounting or ERP software — Priority, SAP, Hashavshevet, iCount/Rivhit, or any accounting system — and pulls and sends the documents herself, right inside the conversation. "Resend the invoice" and she sends it on the spot. "I already paid" and she checks the system and sends back the receipt or account statement. "What's my balance?" and she sends the up-to-date ledger card (account statement) that itemizes exactly what's open.

Because she clears the paperwork friction on her own, the excuse that used to buy a client another three weeks now gets answered in seconds — and the dispute resolves without ever landing on you. Every document goes out under your brand, professional and polite, so the relationship stays intact while the balance gets settled.
