Dynamics 365 Business Central Collections With an AI Agent That Calls

Business Central can issue reminders and finance charge memos. Jess does the conversations: she phones overdue customers in a natural voice, follows up on WhatsApp and email, and keeps going until the invoice is paid.

How do you automate collections in Business Central?

Seenn adds an AI collections agent to Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. Its agent, Jess, takes your open invoices, calls each overdue customer in a natural voice, follows up on WhatsApp and email, and logs every promise to pay. We connect Dynamics 365 on request; until then you upload an Excel export and she starts the same day.

What does Business Central do for collections on its own?

Business Central issues Reminders from reminder terms and levels, with fees, interest and grace periods, and Reminder Automation can create, issue and email them as PDFs on a schedule. Finance charge memos add interest or fees. The PayPal Payments Standard extension puts a PayPal payment link on invoices.

None of it is a conversation. Business Central does not call customers or message them on WhatsApp, and replies to a reminder come back to your team.

What does Jess do with your Dynamics 365 Business Central receivables?

Jess works every open customer ledger entry, not just the accounts someone has time for:

  • Calls first. Jess phones overdue customers in a natural voice, under your company name, handles their answers and ends each call with a next step.
  • Follows up in writing. On official WhatsApp Business from your verified number (Seenn is an approved Meta Tech Provider) and by email under your brand, with a payment link for the exact balance.
  • Sends the paperwork. With a direct connection to Dynamics 365 Business Central, Jess sends invoices and statements when a customer asks for them, in the middle of the conversation.
  • Works by your rules. You set payment terms, grace periods, tone, channel order and when to escalate; she applies them to every account and hands you only what needs a decision.
  • Keeps the record. Every call, message, promise to pay and dispute is logged. The dashboard shows total and overdue receivables, aging that matches your data, what falls due by month and what was collected each month.

How do you export open invoices from Business Central?

  1. Search for Customer Ledger Entries.
  2. Filter Open to Yes, and Document Type to Invoice if you only want invoices.
  3. Optional: use Personalize to show Customer Name, Due Date and Remaining Amount.
  4. Select the Share icon, then Open in Excel. The workbook keeps your filters and columns; upload it to Seenn.

Prefer aging buckets? Run the Aged Accounts Receivable (Excel) report instead; its workbook includes a sheet with the raw data.

Good to know: Customer Name is filled in on ledger entries only when "Copy Customer Name to Entries" is on in Sales & Receivables Setup; otherwise use the customer number. Ledger entries have no email or phone fields, so open your Customers list in Excel as well. Jess needs a phone number and an email for each customer. If the report you export has no contact columns, add them from your customer list before you upload.

Dynamics 365 Business Central collections FAQ

Does Seenn integrate with Dynamics 365 Business Central?

Yes, on request. Tell us you run Dynamics 365 Business Central and we build the direct connection for you. Until it is live, export your open invoices to Excel and upload them, and Jess starts collecting the same day.

Which Business Central data should I export?

Customer Ledger Entries filtered to open entries, using Open in Excel. It gives one row per open invoice with the due date and remaining amount. The Aged Accounts Receivable (Excel) report works too.

Does Seenn also work with Dynamics 365 Finance?

Yes, on request, like Business Central. Tell us which Dynamics 365 product you run and we build the connection; until then, an Excel export of your open customer transactions gets Jess started.

Does my team need to change how it works in Dynamics 365 Business Central?

No. Your team keeps invoicing and posting payments in Dynamics 365 Business Central as usual. Jess works alongside it from the same receivables data and hands your team only the accounts that need a decision.

Which channels does Jess use?

Phone first, in a natural voice. She follows up on official WhatsApp Business from your verified number and by email under your brand, with a payment link for the exact balance. Seenn does not use SMS.

Is our Dynamics 365 Business Central data shared with anyone?

No. Jess learns how your own customers pay so she can collect better for you. Your data stays yours and is never shared with or used for any other business.
